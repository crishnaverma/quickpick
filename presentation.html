Counter Security Slide 8 of 12

Counter Scanner & Anti-Duplicate Fraud

Preventing lost inventory and fraudulent duplicate handovers.

Counter Handover Flow

  1. 1. Customer displays QR pass on phone at the express pickup desk.
  2. 2. Staff scans QR pass or inputs the 16-character token on tablet.
  3. 3. Backend verifies token cryptographic match and status is READY.
  4. 4. Screen displays itemized bag checklist and payment balance due.
  5. 5. Staff clicks 'Confirm Handover': records timestamp, logs staff ID, and marks payment paid.

Fraud & Double-Pickup Shield

Duplicate Handover Blocked

If an already collected QR code is scanned again, the system immediately returns a 400 FRAUD ALERT with exact collection date and staff ID.

Premature Collection Blocked

Orders in PLACED or PREPARING state cannot be collected until staff explicitly marks bags verified and ready.

Zero lost bags • Server-verified cryptographic token matching 8 / 12
Warehouse Integrity Slide 9 of 12

Atomic Inventory Reservation & Audit Logs

Eliminating overselling during peak grocery rushes with database transactions.

Atomic Reservation

Executed inside Prisma $transaction blocks. Available = StockQuantity - ReservedStock. Simultaneous checkouts are strictly verified; stock cannot be oversold.

Prisma $transaction

Automatic Stock Release

When an order is cancelled by customer or rejected by store staff, reserved inventory is automatically released back to stock with structured movement records.

RELEASE Event

Audit Trail Ledger

Every inventory event logs: STOCK_IN, RESERVATION, SALE, RELEASE, ADJUSTMENT, or LOSS with post-balance and staff author.

Full Traceability
Warehouse accuracy • Low stock threshold push alerts 9 / 12
Production Engineering Slide 10 of 12

Critical Production Business Rules

Rigorous engineering standards built directly into the codebase.

Zero Trust on Frontend Pricing

Frontend prices, tax calculations, and subtotals are completely ignored. All line items are recalculated server-side from database prices.

Server-Side Calculation

Idempotency Key Enforcement

Double-clicks and network retries pass an idempotency key. Duplicate submissions return the existing order without duplicate charges or stock holds.

Idempotent Orders

Server-Side Payment Signature Verification

Razorpay checkout orders require HMAC-SHA256 signature verification on the server before payment is marked SUCCESS.

HMAC-SHA256

Strict Rate Limiting & Helmet Headers

Authentication endpoints limited to 30 attempts / 15 mins. Checkout endpoints protected from brute-force bot sweeps.

Rate Limiter
